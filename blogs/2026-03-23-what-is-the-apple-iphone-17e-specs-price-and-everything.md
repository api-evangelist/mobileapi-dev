TL;DR: The iPhone 17e launched on March 11, 2026 starting at $599 with 256GB storage -- double the base capacity of the iPhone 16e at the same price. It runs Apple's A19 chip with 8GB RAM, supports Apple Intelligence, and features the new C1X modem that is up to 2x faster than its predecessor. It is the most affordable iPhone with full AI capabilities.
